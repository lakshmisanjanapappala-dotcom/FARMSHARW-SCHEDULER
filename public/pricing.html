Supported Indian Payment Methods

FarmSharw accommodates rural realities with both cash and digital payment workflows:

  • Cash on Handover / Pickup: Pay directly in person after physical equipment inspection. The owner records collection in their authenticated dashboard.
  • Online Payment Gateway: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), RuPay debit cards, credit cards, and net banking with cryptographic webhook verification.
