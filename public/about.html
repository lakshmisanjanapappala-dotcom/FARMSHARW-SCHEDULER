About FarmSharw Scheduler

Transforming idle agricultural machinery into accessible, productive community assets.

Our Mission

In India, small and marginal farmers account for over 85% of agricultural operational holdings. High-power machinery like 45+ HP tractors, combine harvesters, laser land levellers, and seed drills represent massive capital investments that individual smallholders cannot afford to purchase. Meanwhile, machinery owners often face 8 to 10 months of machine idle time each year.

FarmSharw Scheduler bridges this gap with a modern, trusted equipment rental and idle-time exchange platform. We provide rural communities with reliable discovery, database-enforced conflict-free reservation scheduling, transparent pricing, and regional language support.

The Core Principles

1. Farmer Trust & KYC

Identity verification via Kisan Credit Cards (KCC), Aadhaar, or driving licenses ensures trustworthy handovers.

2. Geodata Privacy

Exact farm locations are never broadcast publicly. Handover addresses are only unlocked upon mutually confirmed bookings.

3. Zero Schedule Double-Booking

PostgreSQL range exclusion constraints prevent conflicting bookings at the database engine level.

4. Fair Economics

Low 5% platform technology fee, with full support for cash on pickup and digital payments.
