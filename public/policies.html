Platform Policies & Guidelines

Legal terms, privacy safeguards, cancellation refund schedules, and dispute management.

1. Terms of Service

FarmSharw Scheduler operates as a specialized peer-to-peer and business-to-farmer agricultural machinery scheduling platform. By accessing or using the platform, participants agree to comply with all applicable Indian agricultural equipment regulations, motor vehicle road transport laws, and safety guidelines.

Equipment owners certify that machinery offered is in roadworthy and mechanically sound operational condition. Renters certify that equipment will be operated strictly by qualified persons with appropriate agricultural vehicle permits.

2. Privacy & Geodata Protection

We treat farmer personal data and farm locations with strict confidentiality:

  • Exact field coordinates and residential landmarks are obscured on public browse and search views.
  • Exact pickup directions are only revealed to verified renters who hold an active, confirmed reservation.
  • Government identity documents uploaded for KYC verification are stored in private Supabase storage buckets and accessible solely by authorized compliance moderators and the document owner.

3. Cancellation & Refund Rules

All refund calculations are determined authoritatively server-side based on the notice interval before scheduled start time:

  • Greater than 48 Hours: 100% refund of base rental fee and 100% of refundable deposit.
  • 24 to 48 Hours: 70% refund of base rental fee and 100% of refundable deposit.
  • Less than 24 Hours: 30% refund of base rental fee and 100% of refundable deposit.
  • Cancellation by Equipment Owner: 100% immediate full refund of all charges to the renter.

4. Dispute Resolution Procedure

If disagreements arise regarding machine cleanliness, handover delays, mechanical breakdown, or unreturned deposits, either participant may file an in-platform Dispute via their booking details screen:

  1. The dispute record captures photo evidence, handover notes, and desired resolutions.
  2. FarmSharw administrative moderators review the audit trail, messaging history, and inspection logs.
  3. Security deposits remain safeguarded in escrow/record until a formal administrative resolution is determined.
